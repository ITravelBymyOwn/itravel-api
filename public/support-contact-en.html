What to include in a support case

01Your username or account email.
02What you were trying to do when the problem occurred.
03City(ies) of the trip and approximate time of the incident.
04Screenshot if it helps explain the problem.
05If it was a payment: date, amount, and available PayPal reference.
06If a file failed: indicate which one did not appear (PDF, CSV, or receipt), the device used, and if you tried the individual backup button.
Do not send

Do not send full card numbers, passwords, verification codes, PayPal credentials, or identity documents unless we legitimately and securely request them for a specific case.

Payment / Generation

Did you pay but not receive an itinerary?

ITBMO retains the necessary progress and attempts to resume a paid generation after a disconnection, interruption, or page reload. Completed cities are retained, and ASTRA only retries pending cities. If the Planner indicates that attempts have been exhausted, do not repeat the purchase: use the available retry option or write to us to review whether a replacement or refund is applicable according to the Terms and applicable law.

Downloads

Didn’t all your documents appear?

Upon completion of a trip, ITBMO prepares the itinerary in PDF format, the Excel-compatible CSV file, and the payment receipt in PDF format. On computers, you can download these files; on mobile devices, ITBMO will attempt to open or share them using the options available in your browser and system. If any file does not appear, use the individual buttons that remain available below the itinerary before closing or reloading the page. Before using Reset
