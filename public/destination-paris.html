How to think about a trip to Paris

Paris rewards a neighborhood-led plan: essential monuments, museums and time to experience the city without turning every day into a race. The goal is not to accumulate places, but to order areas, bookings and transfers so useful time is protected. ITBMO can adapt that structure to your actual arrival, accommodation, pace and priorities.

Essential experiences

  • Île de la Cité and the Seine
  • The Louvre or Musée d’Orsay
  • Eiffel Tower and Trocadéro
  • Montmartre and its streets

They do not all have to fit into one trip. Choose according to season, group energy and available bookings.

How to organize your days

A 4–6-day stay is a reasonable starting point. Place fixed-time activities first, then group everything else by proximity. Avoid repeated transfers and allow room for meals, weather and rest.

ITBMO tip: Book major museums for a fixed time and group each day by riverbank and neighborhood.

Frequently asked questions

How many days should I spend in Paris?

As a reference, 4–6 days allow you to combine the main highlights at a reasonable pace. Your final duration depends on transfers and interests.

What is the best time to visit Paris?

A common window is April–June and September–October, although weather, demand and activities vary. Always confirm conditions for your dates.
