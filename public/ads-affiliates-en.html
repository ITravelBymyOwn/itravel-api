ITBMO seeks to maintain a useful and accessible experience through a combination of route generation revenue, advertising, and affiliate relationships.

This disclosure supplements the Terms of Service and the Privacy & Cookie Policy.

01

How ITBMO Monetizes

ITBMO may earn revenue from: (a) payments for itinerary generation; (b) advertising, including Google AdSense; and (c) commissions derived from affiliate links or tools.

Monetization is designed to be contextually integrated into the experience, for example, in the “For Your Trip” section, during or after itinerary generation.

02

What is an Affiliate Link

An affiliate link contains attribution information that allows a partner to identify that you arrived at their site from ITBMO. If you complete an eligible action, ITBMO may receive a commission.

Important

ITBMO will place clear and understandable notices near relevant retail spaces, ensuring that the disclosure is visible in reasonable proximity to the relevant link, button, or recommendation, so that you do not have to search this page to know that a link may be monetized.

04

Editorial and commercial independence

The existence of a commission does not automatically make a service the only recommended option. ITBMO may display non-affiliated alternatives when they are useful to the traveler.

ITBMO strives to integrate commercial links in a way that does not obscure the nature of the content or create a false relationship with third parties.

05

Third-Party Purchases and Services

When you click on a link and leave ITBMO, any subsequent booking or purchase is made within the third party's environment and is subject to their terms, privacy, cancellation, refund, change, customer service, and availability policies.

06

Google AdSense

ITBMO may display ads served through Google AdSense. Ads may be contextual, measured, or personalized where permitted by law and your consent choices.

The appearance of an ad does not imply that ITBMO has evaluated, approved, or endorsed the advertiser or its products. Interactions with third-party advertisers are the responsibility of the user and the third party, without prejudice to applicable legal rights.

07

Tracking, Attribution, and Cookies

Affiliate partners and advertising technology providers may use tracking links, cookies, identifiers, or other attribution mechanisms.

When the law requires consent for non-core technologies, ITBMO will endeavor to manage those technologies using the appropriate consent solution. When Google AdSense is enabled for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, and Google's requirements apply, a certified CMP compliant with the required technical framework will be used. In jurisdictions where technical opt-out signals must be recognized, ITBMO will endeavor to respect them when the Service becomes subject to that obligation. Privacy & Cookies.

For California consumers, where the CCPA or other applicable regulations deem certain advertising, attribution, or transfer as "sale" or "sharing," ITBMO will implement legally required opt-out mechanisms and recognize signals such as Global Privacy Control (GPC) where appropriate.

08

Partner Brands, Logos, and Materials

The names, brands, logos, and materials of KAYAK, Travelpayouts, Skyscanner, Booking.com, GetYourGuide, Viator, Omio, Airalo, Holafly, Google, PayPal, and other third parties belong to their respective owners.

09

Changes to Business Relationships

Affiliate programs, commission rates, tools, authorized brands, and terms are subject to change. ITBMO may modify or deactivate partners without prior notice when necessary.
