Before you travel

ITBMO plans. You confirm.

Real-world information changes. A final check protects your experience, especially when a booking depends on an exact schedule.

◷Estimated times

Transfers are references that you should confirm with up-to-date navigation; activity durations are approximate and can be adjusted to your pace.

▣Schedules and bookings

Confirm opening hours, availability, tickets, prices, and conditions with the provider.

⇄Flights, trains, and tours

Check schedules, terminals, departure points, and requirements directly with the operator.

☼Weather and outdoors

Check up-to-date conditions before weather-sensitive activities.

◎Routes and navigation

Use up-to-date navigation to confirm your route before traveling.

◇Official Requirements

Visas, documentation, health requirements, and alerts must be verified with official sources.

!

Especially important: Never rely solely on an estimated travel time to catch a flight, train, ferry, tour, or activity with a fixed schedule. Allow sufficient time.
