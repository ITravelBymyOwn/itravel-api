Artificial Intelligence and Variability

ASTRA and Info Chat use artificial intelligence. By their nature, two identical requests may produce different results. Outputs may contain inaccuracies, omissions, errors, or outdated information.

ITBMO designs ASTRA to prioritize geographical logic, must-see attractions, traveler context, accommodation as a reference point, and route efficiency, but it does not guarantee that one generation of a specific route will be identical to another, that it will include a specific location, or that it will represent the only or best possible route.

The Planner allows multilingual interaction in languages ​​supported by the chat. Availability, language detection, and quality may vary depending on the language. If a language is not supported, ASTRA may indicate this during the conversation. The language used for the conversation does not necessarily determine the final itinerary language, which is selected later within the planning flow.

Your obligation to verify critical information

ASTRA plans. You confirm.

Do not rely solely on the itinerary for decisions that may affect connections, security, documentation, money, or legal compliance.

Before traveling or booking, you must verify directly with current sources, operators, or authorities, as appropriate:

  • opening and operating hours;
  • prices, availability, tickets, and reservations;

  • Weather and outdoor activity conditions;

Never use an estimated travel time as the sole basis for arriving at an activity with a fixed schedule. Add sufficient margin.
