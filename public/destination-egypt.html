How to think about a trip to Egypt

A first trip to Egypt often connects Cairo, Luxor and Aswan. Transfers and early visits are central to the plan. The goal is not to accumulate places, but to order areas, bookings and transfers so useful time is protected. ITBMO can adapt that structure to your actual arrival, accommodation, pace and priorities.

Essential experiences

  • Giza Pyramids and Saqqara
  • Cairo museums
  • Luxor temples and tombs
  • Aswan and Abu Simbel

They do not all have to fit into one trip. Choose according to season, group energy and available bookings.

How to organize your days

A 7–12-day stay is a reasonable starting point. Place fixed-time activities first, then group everything else by proximity. Avoid repeated transfers and allow room for meals, weather and rest.

ITBMO tip: Protect cooler hours, coordinate guides and transfers and verify official conditions before travel.

Frequently asked questions

How many days should I spend in Egypt?

As a reference, 7–12 days allow you to combine the main highlights at a reasonable pace. Your final duration depends on transfers and interests.

What is the best time to visit Egypt?

A common window is October–April, although weather, demand and activities vary. Always confirm conditions for your dates.
