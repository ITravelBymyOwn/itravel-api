The complete process

Four moments.
You stay in control.

01

Create your space

Sign up, sign in or continue as a guest. Your account helps you access trips across devices.

  • The amount is confirmed before itinerary generation begins.
  • Confirmation email may take a few minutes.
02

Build the route

Add travelers, cities, dates and day trips. Let ITBMO propose the structure or include known flights, hotels, bookings and fixed times.

  • Up to 30 effective days in one generation.
  • Multiple cities, regional stays and day trips.
03

Review the price and generate

Checkout appears only when the route is ready. It is a one-time charge processed through PayPal, with no subscription or automatic renewal.

  • Current promotional price: US$2.99.
  • A valid interrupted paid generation keeps its entitlement.
04

Explore, download and prepare

Receive a structured itinerary by day and city, then use the workspace to navigate and prepare.

  • PDF, editable Excel, CSV and receipt.
  • My Trips, Google Maps/Waze routes and For your trip.
What ITBMO uses

More context creates
a better route.

You can start with very little, but every relevant detail makes the result feel more like your way of traveling.

PEOPLE

Who is traveling

Traveler count, ages, children, mobility, pace and needs that must be protected.

TIME

When and how long

Dates, arrival and departure times, confirmed bookings and the real window available each day.

INTERESTS

What matters to you

History, food, nature, art, shopping, nightlife or places that cannot be missed.

LOGISTICS

What is already fixed

Hotels, stations, airports, transfers and conditions the itinerary should never ignore.

Inside the engine

Technology proposes.
Structure verifies.

ITBMO combines AI with its own coverage, continuity, timing and consistency controls. Times, closures, requirements and availability can still change, so always confirm before booking.

TRANSPARENCY

A decision aid, not an impossible promise

ITBMO does not book on your behalf. It organizes the landscape so you reach decisions with a coherent route and far less manual work.
