GET MORE FROM YOUR ITINERARY

Three things to consider before generating.

The videos show you how. These decisions help make the result useful for your trip.

01

Protect what is already fixed

If you booked a flight, ticket or tour, include its date and time. A preference is flexible; a confirmed booking is a real commitment.

02

Prioritize what matters

Highlight the places you are most excited about and your travelers’ needs. A short list of priorities leaves room to discover and rest.

03

Delegate with confidence

No lodging yet or unfamiliar with local transport? Use the recommendation options. Fields marked optional can stay empty.

The thinking behind the route

Technology proposes.
Structure verifies.

ITBMO uses your trip context and its own coverage, continuity, timing and consistency controls to build a more useful route. Times, closures, requirements and availability can still change, so always confirm before booking.

TRANSPARENCY

A decision aid, not an impossible promise

ITBMO does not book on your behalf. It organizes the landscape so you reach decisions with a coherent route and far less manual work.
