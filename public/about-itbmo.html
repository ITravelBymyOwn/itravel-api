I Travel By My Own

The freedom to travel your way.
Without planning everything alone.

ITBMO began with a simple idea: technology should help you organize a trip that feels genuinely yours, without taking away control or turning planning into another job.

✦
Our mission

Reduce complexity. Protect what makes a trip personal.

Dates, cities, bookings, distance, interests and pace all compete for space. ITBMO organizes them into a clear route so you spend more energy experiencing the trip and less time reconciling dozens of tabs.

Product principles

What we want you to feel
every time you use it.

01

This feels like me

The route responds to your dates, interests, pace and constraints—not a generic template.

02

This is actually doable

The structure protects continuity, time and geography to reduce impossible days.

03

I am still in control

ITBMO helps you decide. You confirm availability, purchases and bookings with each provider.

Technology with a purpose

AI is part of the engine.
It is not the whole answer.

On top of it, we build rules, audits and tools designed to produce a more consistent and useful result.

◎

Context first

The system starts from what you tell it, not one itinerary copied for everyone.

⌁

Continuity

Each day should connect its points and respect actual movement.

◷

Observable quality

Internal controls review coverage, structure and consistency before publishing.

↗

Continuous evolution

Traveler feedback helps detect where the experience needs to improve.

A connected platform

From the first idea
to the day you travel.

ITBMO's vision reaches beyond generating a document. The workspace, routes, My Trips and For your trip create a foundation designed to support you before and during the journey.

GLOBAL AND BILINGUAL

Trips without a single border

The experience works in English and Spanish for itineraries around the world.

TRANSPARENT MODEL

One payment for each itinerary generated

No required membership. Some recommendations may use affiliate links, identified in the relevant legal information.

RESPONSIBILITY

Confirmation still matters

Schedules, prices, entry rules and availability change. ITBMO organizes; each provider confirms.

PRIVACY

Information in service of the trip

The platform uses the data needed to deliver and improve the service according to its terms and privacy policy.
